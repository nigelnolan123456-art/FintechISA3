Features

Everything Pocketwise does, and two calculators you can use right now.

📊

Spending tracker

Payments from your bank, cards and UPI are sorted into categories automatically. Change a category once and Pocketwise remembers it.

💼

Monthly budgets

Set a limit for food, travel or shopping. You get an alert at 80% and again at 100%.

🎯

Savings goals

Name a goal, set an amount and a date, and see how much to save each month.

🔔

Bill reminders

Rent, subscriptions and card bills each get a reminder before the due date.

🏦

Loan planner

See the EMI and total interest for a loan before you apply for it.

🔒

Read-only and private

Pocketwise can view your accounts but never move money. You can unlink an account at any time.

Free calculators
