About Pocketwise

A personal finance app idea for people who earn or spend their first real money.

The problem

Many young people get pocket money, a stipend or a first salary and reach the end of the month without knowing where it went. Bank apps show transactions, but not what they add up to.

Spreadsheets work, but few people keep them up to date.

Our approach

Pocketwise sorts spending automatically, shows one clear picture of the month and turns goals into a monthly amount you can act on.

The app has read-only access to accounts. It can show your money but cannot move it.

FinTech theme: Personal Finance Management

Pocketwise belongs to the personal finance management (PFM) category. Existing apps in this space, such as Walnut, Money Manager and Mint, were used as references for structure and features. All content here is original dummy content.
