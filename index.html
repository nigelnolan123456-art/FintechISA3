    What you can do with Pocketwise

    📊

    Track spending

    Every payment is sorted into a category, so you see rent, food and travel at a glance.

    🎯

    Save for goals

    Set a target and a date. Pocketwise tells you how much to put away each month.

    🏦

    Plan loans

    Check the monthly EMI and total interest before you borrow.

    🔔

    Get bill alerts

    Reminders arrive before rent, subscriptions and card bills are due.

    Start in three steps

    1. Create your account

      Sign up with your email or phone number.

    2. Link your bank or UPI

      Choose the accounts you want to see. Read-only access, so Pocketwise can never move money.

    3. Set a budget

      Pick a monthly limit per category and get a nudge when you get close.
